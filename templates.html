CERTIFICATE TEMPLATE REQUIREMENTS

  1. Upload a PNG or JPG certificate template background image.
  2. Recommended orientation: Landscape.
  3. Recommended resolution: at least 1600 × 1100 px.
  4. The certificate background should have appropriate blank areas for generated text and QR codes.
  5. The template layout must allow space for:
    • • Participant Name
    • • Certificate Description
    • • Date
    • • QR Code
    • • Verification ID
  6. Existing logos, borders, lines, signatures, and decorations are allowed and will remain part of the background.
  7. Do not include participant-specific information in the template background image.
